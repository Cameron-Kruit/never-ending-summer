Roles in sewer society:
Artisan - focuses on engineering, crafting, repairs, etc. Doesn't venture outside.
Student - focuses on knowledge from the old world. Rarely ventures outside for artifacts and data. Everyone is a student till a certain age, but dedicated students just learn more stuff.
Cartographer - A student who often joins scouts with the intent of mapping the outside world.
Scout - goes outside scavenging and foraging for supplies
Hunter - primarily focusses on trapping, tracking, and hunting animals
Elder - main responsibilities are keeping track of the date, teaching and maintaining the knowledge of the old society, provide leadership and resolve conflicts. Doesn't venture outside. This position has been created after the death of the teachers, since there is no one to bring knowledge from the old society. Also due to the sacrifice of all the older kids, it's been decided that the young must now sacrifice themselves first since without adults, there will be no one who can repopulate humanity.
